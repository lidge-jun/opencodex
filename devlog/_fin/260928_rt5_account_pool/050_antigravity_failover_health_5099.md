## Current `dev` and conflict resolution

- Generic OAuth 429 is presence-driven, excludes Codex/Anthropic, and keeps process-local cooldowns (`src/oauth/generic-account-failover.ts:48-57,76-97,192-220`). #6106's pause handling is present in candidate selection (`:244-279`).
- #6132/#6133 already landed bounded Antigravity 401 sibling recovery in primary Google adapter dispatch, after same-account refresh or terminal refresh failure; it preserves the paired token/project snapshot and charges a credential hop (`src/server/responses/adapter-dispatch.ts:596-640,652-670,760-765`; `structure/transports/inventory.md:44`). Do not broaden 401 to continuations, passthrough, sidecars, or after output in this train.
- The PR's “dynamic scaling” is already implemented differently and more tightly on `dev`: ingress snapshots eligible accounts, funds at most six and sets `max(3, fundedAccounts - 1)` rotations (`src/server/responses/request-transport.ts:814-823`); `src/oauth/generic-account-failover.ts:49-57` caps the roster. The PR's separate `genericOAuthMaxFailovers` is therefore redundant and can increase the request cost beyond this contract.
- Google adapter 403 normalization already reads at most 4096 bytes with 2-second total/first-byte/inactivity deadlines and forwards a fixed validation prefix only for a complete structured reason (`src/adapters/google-http.ts:32-49`; `src/adapters/google-errors.ts:4-15,112-119`). A plain string, unrelated `PERMISSION_DENIED`, oversized/incomplete body, or failed read must retain its 403.
- `git merge-tree --write-tree origin/dev refs/rt5/pr-5099` reports content conflicts in `src/oauth/generic-account-failover.ts`, four Responses dispatch/continuation files plus sidecar, `structure/transports/inventory.md`, and `tests/oauth/generic-oauth-failover.test.ts`. Resolve by **not merging that tree**: keep current 401/429 selection and budget code, add only the 403 classifier/gate below, and rewrite affected contract text against current `dev`. The source branch's older `src/server/index.ts`, lifecycle, and `src/types/provider.ts` additions do not enter this slice.
